I'm building **Study Hub**, a browser-based 3D virtual office for my class: people walk around as cute chibi anime avatars, sit at desks, present on a big screen, chat and use office items. It runs in Three.js, and **every avatar and every piece of furniture is built in code from simple 3D shapes** (spheres, tapered capsules, rounded boxes, cylinders, lathe/turned profiles). There are no imported 3D model files.

I've attached a zip with **everything the app has right now**: renders of the avatars, all eye and mouth styles, expressions, hairstyles, clothes, items, actions and the current office, plus measured spec sheets. Please use it as the starting point. Keep what works and redesign what looks weak.

**What looks weak right now:**
- The hair reads like a helmet with leaf-shaped bangs. It needs much more variety and more natural anime clumps.
- The bodies are stiff and doll-like.
- The face kit is small.
- The office is plain.

Please design the following as clear, labelled design sheets I can rebuild in code.

## 1. Chibi base models: female and male
- Full turnaround for each, in a neutral **A-pose** on a plain light background: **front, 3/4, side, back**. Show a grey "base mannequin" version (no hair, simple underwear-like shorts/top) **and** a dressed example.
- **Proportions:** about 2.5 to 3 heads tall, with the head roughly 1/3 of the total height. The cute anime chibi look is a big head, small rounded body, short limbs and small hands and feet.
  - Use `spec/proportions.png` as the baseline. The units are metres in the 3D scene: ground 0, knee 0.30, hip joint 0.60, shoulder joint 0.99, head centre 1.35, head radius 0.30, top of head ~1.62.
  - Keep the same scale, or propose better numbers and write them on the sheet.
- **Mark every joint** (neck, shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, ankle) with its height and position, because the app animates them.
- Female and male should differ clearly but stay cute:
  - Female: narrower shoulders, small waist, wider hips.
  - Male: broader, straighter torso, slightly thicker limbs.
- Show **body types** slim / regular / broad, and **8 head shapes**: round, oval, soft square, heart, chubby mochi, long, diamond, bean.
- Describe each body part as a simple shape with dimensions, for example "upper arm = tapered capsule, top radius 0.036, bottom 0.030, length 0.155". I need to be able to rebuild it from primitives.
- Hands: simple mitten-like hands with a thumb and 3 to 4 short fingers. Ears: small and rounded.

## Style guide
- Original designs only. Cute anime chibi, soft and rounded, toon / soft shading, friendly and cozy. Don't copy existing characters or games.
- One shared **colour palette with hex codes** for skin tones (10), hair (16), clothes and the office.
- Everything should be readable at small size, because avatars are seen from a third-person camera a few metres away.

## Output format
- Labelled PNG sheets on a light background, with a scale grid in metres.
- Every option has a name and a short id. Keep the existing ids where a style already exists; see `spec/avatar-options.json`.
- A short written spec under each sheet with the shapes and numbers I need to rebuild it in Three.js.
